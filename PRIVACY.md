# Privacy Policy — LCC GradeTrack

Last updated: November 18, 2026

LCC GradeTrack does not collect, transmit or share any personal data.

## What the app stores

The profile you enter (name, student number, program, year level), your terms, subjects, scores and settings. All of it is stored only on your phone, in the app's private storage (an SQLite database and app preferences).

An optional PIN is stored as a salted hash, never as the PIN itself.

## What the app does not do

- It has no account or sign-in.
- It does not request the Internet permission and cannot send data anywhere.
- It contains no analytics, advertising or crash-reporting services.
- It is excluded from Android cloud backup and device-to-device transfer.

## Permissions

| Permission | Why |
|---|---|
| Notifications | Optional reminders before upcoming assessments. Off by default. |
| Run at startup | Restores scheduled reminders after the phone restarts. |
| Biometrics | Optional fingerprint unlock. Handled by Android; the app never sees fingerprint data. |
| Vibrate | Haptic feedback. |

## Exports

When you export a backup, CSV or PDF, the file is handed to the Android share sheet. Where it goes after that (a drive, a chat, email) is your choice. Backups do not include your PIN.

## Deleting your data

Settings › Backup & export › Delete all data, or uninstall the app. Either removes everything the app stored.
